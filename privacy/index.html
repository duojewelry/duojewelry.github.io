Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Duo Jewelry does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information.

  • The boxes you design (colors, text, messages and ring choices) are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • The app does not use the network.
  • The app reads the device's motion sensors only to make the ring sparkle as you tilt the phone. This data never leaves the device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
