Duo Jewelry

Turn your iPhone Duo into an engagement ring box.

How it works

  • Design the box: velvet color, the name on the lid, your message, the ring and a tune.
  • Tap Go, close your iPhone Duo and hand it over.
  • When they open it, the lid lifts, the music box plays and the ring waits in the cushion. Tap the ring to lift it out.

Questions

How do I get back to the settings from the box?
Press and hold anywhere on the screen for about 2.5 seconds.

Does it work on other iPhones?
Yes. On iPhones without a fold, tap the closed box to open it. The app is made for iPhone Duo, where folding the phone open opens the box.

Where are my boxes saved?
Only on your phone. The app has no account and doesn't use the network. Deleting the app deletes your boxes.

Is the ring real?
The ring in the app is virtual, so you'll still need the real one.
